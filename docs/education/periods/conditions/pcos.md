# PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome)

A common hormonal and metabolic condition affecting roughly **8–13%** of women of reproductive age, with a large share undiagnosed. Diagnosed by having **two of three**: irregular or absent ovulation, signs of excess androgens (acne, excess facial/body hair, scalp hair thinning), and polycystic-appearing ovaries on ultrasound.

Often involves insulin resistance, and carries elevated long-term risk of type 2 diabetes and cardiovascular disease — so it's a whole-body condition, not just a period problem. Management includes lifestyle measures, hormonal contraception for cycle regulation, metformin, and ovulation induction if trying to conceive.

Infrequent periods with PCOS means the endometrium can build up without regular shedding, which raises endometrial cancer risk over time — one reason it's actively managed rather than ignored.
