# Amenorrhoea

**Primary** — no period by age **15**, or by **13** with no other signs of puberty. **Secondary** — periods stop for **3+ months** in someone who previously had them.

## Causes

Pregnancy (always rule out first), breastfeeding, contraception, PCOS, thyroid disease, high prolactin, **functional hypothalamic amenorrhoea** (from low energy availability — undereating, over-exercising, significant stress, or low body weight), premature ovarian insufficiency, and structural causes.

## Functional hypothalamic amenorrhoea

Deserves emphasis because it's often treated as a badge of athletic dedication when it's actually a signal the body has switched off reproduction to conserve energy — and it carries real consequences for bone density. It's a core component of **RED-S** (Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport).
