# Other conditions worth knowing

- **Endometrial and cervical polyps** — benign growths causing bleeding between periods
- **PID (pelvic inflammatory disease)** — infection of the reproductive organs; causes pain, unusual discharge, fever. Needs prompt antibiotics to prevent lasting damage
- **Thyroid disorders** — both under- and overactive thyroid disrupt cycles, and are easily tested for
- **Bleeding disorders** — worth considering if heavy periods have been the norm since menarche, especially with easy bruising or nosebleeds
