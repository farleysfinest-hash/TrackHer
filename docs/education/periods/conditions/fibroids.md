# Fibroids (leiomyomas)

Benign muscular growths in or on the uterus. Extremely common — a majority of women develop them by age **50**, though many cause no symptoms. When symptomatic: heavy or prolonged bleeding, pelvic pressure, frequent urination, back pain. Treatments range from medication to targeted procedures (uterine artery embolisation, myomectomy) to hysterectomy.
