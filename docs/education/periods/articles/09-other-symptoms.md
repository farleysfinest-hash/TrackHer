# Other symptoms across the cycle

*Gut, head, skin, sleep, and conditions that ride the same hormonal wave.*

## Digestive changes

Prostaglandins affect the bowel too, so looser stools or diarrhoea around day 1 are common — as is constipation in the luteal phase, when progesterone slows gut motility. People with IBS often find symptoms track the cycle.

## Migraine

Menstrual migraine is triggered by the drop in oestrogen just before the period. It's typically more severe and less responsive to usual treatment than other migraines. If you get migraine *with aura*, that's important information for contraception choice — combined oestrogen pills are generally avoided due to stroke risk.

## Skin

Acne often flares in the late luteal phase as oestrogen falls and androgens are relatively higher.

## Breasts

Tenderness and fullness (cyclical mastalgia) is common from ovulation onward and resolves with bleeding.

## Sleep and temperature

Progesterone raises core body temperature in the luteal phase, which for some people disrupts sleep.

## Cognition and mood

Effects are real but individually variable and often overstated. Many people report sharper verbal fluency mid-cycle and lower stress tolerance premenstrually.

## Existing conditions

Asthma, epilepsy (**catamenial epilepsy**), depression, and autoimmune conditions can all fluctuate with the cycle. If yours does, tracking it alongside your cycle gives your doctor something concrete to work with.
